---
name: Video preview proportions
description: Portrait-film previews may remain inside a wide canvas iframe.
---

Do not assume portrait video metadata changes the canvas iframe's dimensions. Keep a portrait film fitted inside the available preview and size its type and artwork relative to the film, not the outer viewport.

**Why:** The user reported a crowded logo, name, and download ending because the 9:16 film was displayed in a 1920×1080 canvas iframe.

**How to apply:** When revising the promotional film, preserve both the true vertical export and the fitted wide-preview presentation. Verify the ending in the actual wide preview as well as a portrait frame.
