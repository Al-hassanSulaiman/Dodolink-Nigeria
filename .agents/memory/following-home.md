---
name: Following-first Home
description: User intent for Instagram-style store following and discovery on Home.
---

Home should feel like following stores on Instagram, not a fixed best-rated
catalogue. Prioritize followed stores and their products, but still expose some
popular unfollowed stores. The user explicitly rejected fixed totals of five
stores and six products. Refresh should rotate the selection, and shoppers
should be able to continue browsing more eligible stores/products.

**Why:** The user wants customers to keep up with stores they choose while
allowing stores with many followers to receive a little extra visibility.

**How to apply:** Keep circular store photos with shortened names in a
horizontally swipeable row at the top of Home. Do not replace following-based
discovery with review-only rankings or interpret following as an exclusive feed
that prevents discovering other stores.
