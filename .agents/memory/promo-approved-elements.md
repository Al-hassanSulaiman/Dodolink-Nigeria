The user approved the promotional film's visuals and music and requested that a face-only correction not change anything else.

**Why:** The user said “everything is perfect” and explicitly emphasized “don't change any other thing” and “this the only correction.”

**How to apply:** Limit subsequent targeted corrections to the requested element. Keep all other approved imagery, outfits, poses, text, background, soundtrack, timing, and transitions unchanged unless the user explicitly requests a change.

For strictly localized image corrections, do not replace the whole approved image with an AI edit.

**Why:** Even a face-only editing prompt changed surrounding details in the generated candidate.

**How to apply:** Composite only the corrected area through a localized mask onto the original, and verify that pixels outside the mask are unchanged.
