---
name: Generated image proportions
description: Image edits may ignore a requested portrait aspect ratio.
---

Verify an image edit's actual dimensions before placing it in a portrait film. Preserve all faces when fitting a multi-person photograph; do not assume a cover crop will keep everyone visible.

**Why:** An image edit returned a square image despite a portrait reference board and an explicit vertical-composition prompt.

**How to apply:** Inspect the output first. When the aspect ratio differs, fit the full photograph into a portrait composition with deliberate background extension and space for titles, rather than blindly cropping its sides.
