---
name: Portfolio analytics privacy
description: Privacy and attribution boundaries for the portfolio's client-interest tracking
---

Use first-party, cookie-free page-load analytics, not persistent visitor identification. Client attribution means “this private shared link was used,” never proof that the intended recipient visited. Anonymous page loads must not be joined into person profiles.

**Why:** The portfolio task requires insight into client interest without cookie banners. Random client-link codes offer optional attribution without putting private client labels into URLs or adding fingerprinting.

**How to apply:** Keep private labels in the admin-only dashboard. Do not collect stored IP addresses, full URLs, referrers, browser fingerprints, or contact form contents as analytics dimensions. Respect Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. Describe measured duration as visible-page time (which can include idle time), not active attention.
