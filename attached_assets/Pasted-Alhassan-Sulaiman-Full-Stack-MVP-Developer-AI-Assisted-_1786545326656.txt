DodoLink NG
Nigerian Marketplace Platform

  DodoLink NG is a marketplace platform designed to connect customers with businesses across Nigeria, allowing users to discover stores 
and products and interact with businesses through a single platform.
  Finding and interacting with local businesses can be fragmented. Customers may need to search across different platforms, contact sellers 
separately, and manage different parts of the buying process independently. 
 DodoLink NG brings these interactions into one marketplace experience. DodoLink NG provides a centralized platform where customers can discover 
businesses and their products, while businesses can establish their own storefronts and manage their presence on the platform.
